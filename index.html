How to Sync with Google Sheets

To review your journal data in a Google Sheet file:

  1. Click Data Menu → Export Excel (CSV) in the top menu to download your trades.
  2. Open your Google Drive and open or create your Master Trading Journal sheet.
  3. Go to File → Import → Upload and select the downloaded CSV file.
  4. Select "Replace current sheet" or "Insert new sheet" to update your records!
